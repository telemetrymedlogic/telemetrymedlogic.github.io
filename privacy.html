Telemetry USMLE Privacy Policy

Telemetry MedLogic provides Telemetry USMLE for medical education. This policy describes how the production iOS app handles your information.

Effective November 9, 2026.

Study data on your device

Telemetry keeps your local profiles, answers, study progress, bookmarks, notes, settings, reading positions, and saved AI study material in the app on your device. Study features use local profiles. The current production app does not send your study activity to Telemetry MedLogic, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Private PDF Library

When you select a document in the iOS Files picker, Telemetry makes a private copy for the active local profile. If the file is stored in iCloud Drive or another Files provider, that provider may download the file for your import. Telemetry stores its copy and associated reading data inside the app and does not upload the document to a Telemetry server. You may choose to export profile files to a location you control.

AI Study and Read Aloud

On supported devices, AI Study uses Apple’s on-device model. Standard Read Aloud uses iOS speech synthesis. Telemetry’s cloud AI and cloud voice services are disabled in the current production build.

Web links and support

When you open a website link, the destination receives the usual browser request and applies its own privacy policy. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and handle your request. Please leave patient records and sensitive documents out of support emails.

Keeping and deleting your data

Study data stays on your device while you use the app. Deleting a PDF from My Private Library removes the app’s copy and its associated reading records. The original stays in the location you imported it from. You can clear AI conversation history separately and reset saved reading positions in Settings. To remove Telemetry’s local data, use Delete App in iOS. Copies you exported and any iOS or computer backups are managed separately.

Privacy questions

Contact Telemetry MedLogic at telemetrymedlogic@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. We will update this policy when the app’s privacy practices change.
