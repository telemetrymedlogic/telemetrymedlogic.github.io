Telemetry MedLogic Support

How can we help?

Our support team can help with study questions, reading features, bookmarks, saved progress, and technical problems.

Contact Support

Email our team:

telemetrymedlogic@gmail.com

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the problem.

Please do not send passwords, medical records, or copyrighted study PDFs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a question error?

Email the question ID, subject, and a short explanation of the suspected error.

How do I find my saved questions?

Use the saved-question review features available in Telemetry USMLE.

How do I resume PDF reading?

Open your document from the library. Use the reader navigation and bookmark controls.

How do I contact you about privacy?

Email telemetrymedlogic@gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line.
