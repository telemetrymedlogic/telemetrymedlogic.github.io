Help Center

We're here to help.

Need help with Telemetry USMLE? We're here to assist with questions, study progress, bookmarks, PDF reading, and technical issues.

Contact Support

Email: telemetrymedlogic@gmail.com

When contacting support, please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a brief description of the problem.

Please do not send passwords, personal medical records, or copyrighted study PDFs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an incorrect question?

Email us the question ID, subject, and a description of the suspected error.

How do I recover my reading position?

Open the document from your library and use the reader navigation controls. If the issue continues, contact support.

How do I ask about privacy or my data?

Email telemetrymedlogic@gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
